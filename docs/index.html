Hardy Home · house-bot

A private assistant for household life.

Find the email you need, review your calendar, and organize everyday household information with a self-hosted assistant.

Google connections are optional. Each account uses the permissions its owner approves, with access controlled by the private household application.

Purpose and access

Useful information, within the access you grant.

Hardy Home is operated for a private household. The public website explains the project and its data practices; access to the application requires the household operator's authorization.

Find and understand email

Search approved Gmail messages and request summaries or answers grounded in their contents.

Review calendar events

Read the calendar information made available by an approved connection, including event details.

Organize household information

Use the private assistant to keep track of authorized notes, lists and other household information.

Local assistant processing. The household deployment runs its AI locally. A protected service uses Bitwarden for Google credentials, keeping credential values out of the assistant's prompts. Requested account content can become part of retained conversations and artifacts.

Read the privacy policy for access, processing, retention, sharing and deletion details.
