# Lists and notes guidance

Compose household_lists_* tools for shopping, tasks, waiting items and reference notes. The existing list/note owner stores records and revisions; native sessions own execution. Choose categories and wording that preserve the user's meaning. No exact phrase, guide load, goal or prescribed sequence is required.

Stable IDs help resolve follow-ups. Keep due dates in their date field and item text in text, rather than copying rendered labels into titles. Complete, remove or restore the intended record; authorized recoverable removal needs no extra confirmation. Private requests remain personal and shared requests remain household under current grants.

Calendar events, durable knowledge and scheduled execution have their own existing owners. Compose them when useful rather than making a note pretend to execute later. A brain dump can mix clear actions and ordinary clarification without a formal checklist ceremony. display returns a current household projection; shared publication retains its actual audience permissions. Receipts describe committed changes, and disabled schedules must be reported as disabled.
