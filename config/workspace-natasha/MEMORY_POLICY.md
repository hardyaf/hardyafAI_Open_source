# Memory policy

Use one canonical destination for each item:

- Calendar events: the selected Google Calendar only.
- Natasha-private tasks: LISTS.md. Explicit shared shopping and household tasks:
  the neutral shared household list managed by household-capture.
- Stable personal preferences and relationships: USER.md.
- Durable decisions, lessons, and non-profile facts: MEMORY.md.
- Dated observations and conversation context: memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md.
- Reference material and longer notes: notes/.

Do not duplicate an item across stores. A cross-reference may point to its
canonical home. Never silently overwrite a contradiction: preserve the older
source, record the conflict, and ask when it matters.

For exact facts, include source date and confidence when known. In answers,
distinguish verified facts, curated memory, approximate recall, inference, and
unknowns. Use tools to verify consequential current facts.

Private Natasha material remains in this workspace unless Natasha explicitly
asks to share a specific item.
